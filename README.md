## Overview

BagTrace is a concept platform designed to improve transparency and tracking for airline baggage throughout the passenger travel journey.

Lost or delayed baggage remains one of the most common frustrations in air travel. Passengers often have limited visibility into where their luggage is located or when it will arrive.

BagTrace explores how real-time data, predictive analytics, and modern tracking tools could improve baggage visibility and reduce uncertainty for both passengers and airlines.

---

## Problem

Millions of airline bags are delayed or mishandled each year.

Passengers frequently experience:

- Lack of visibility into baggage location
- Delays in baggage delivery after flights
- Difficulty tracking bags across multiple connecting flights
- Limited updates from airline baggage systems
- Stress and inconvenience during travel disruptions

Airlines also face operational challenges when locating and rerouting mishandled baggage.

---

## Concept

BagTrace aims to provide a **modern baggage tracking platform** that improves transparency for both passengers and airline operations teams.

The platform would combine baggage scanning events, flight data, and operational analytics to provide clearer visibility into where baggage is located and how it is moving through the system.

Instead of passengers waiting for updates, BagTrace would provide **real-time baggage tracking insights**.
